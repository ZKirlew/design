Hi, I'm Zayna!

I'm a graphic designer who makes technical and health information clear and easy to follow: packaging and brand systems, process diagrams, illustrations, infographics and presentations. I work in 3D robotic surgery imaging at Stryker alongside engineering and quality teams, so I know medical devices up close, and I design for legibility first, from WCAG contrast to clear type hierarchy.

Coucou: a brand you can spot in a pastry window

A complete brand and packaging system for a French patisserie concept, from first sketches to bags, boxes and tableware. Two rounds of customer feedback turned a hard-to-read pastry-dog into a friendly Shiba Inu chef. The palette was tested against WCAG contrast, so colors that failed as text became fills and backgrounds instead.
