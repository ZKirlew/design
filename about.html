About me

Making health information clear and calm.

I'm a graphic designer who loves turning complicated information into visuals people can understand quickly: brand and packaging systems, illustration, diagrams, infographics and presentations. When the subject is someone's health, I think clarity is a form of care.

My path is a little unusual. I studied Digital Arts & Sciences at the University of Florida, painted and modeled 3D worlds for VR games, and today I work in 3D robotic surgery imaging at Stryker. Working alongside clinical technology every day has shown me how much depends on information being easy to read and hard to misread.

I design with accessibility from the start: color contrast checked against WCAG, readable type hierarchies, and meaning that never depends on color alone.

Based in South Florida
